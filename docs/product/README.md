# Product Documentation

Store feature requirements, phases, page requirements, and launch checklists
here.

Primary platform areas:

- News and video media
- Athlete accounts and profiles
- Published competition standings and verified statistics
- Worldwide competition directory
- Affiliate registration and ticket links
- Cali Central-owned competitions
- User accounts
- Content submissions
- Advertising and sponsorship
- Merchandise

## Global platform principles

- Cali Central covers calisthenics worldwide; California may be a content
  location but is not the platform boundary.
- Member, athlete, organizer, and contributor capabilities belong to one
  account architecture and remain separate from privileged editorial roles.
- User submissions are proposals and never become authoritative public content
  automatically.
- Verification is scoped to an identity connection, profile review, individual
  result, social account, or organizer record rather than one blanket badge.
- Public search includes only published stories, athletes, competitions, and
  videos.
- Newsletter interests remain an honest future-facing interface until a
  reviewed delivery provider and consent lifecycle exist.
