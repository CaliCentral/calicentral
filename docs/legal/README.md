# Legal Drafts

This folder contains working drafts only.

Possible documents include:

- Terms of service
- Privacy policy
- Cookie policy
- Affiliate disclosure
- Editorial policy
- DMCA policy
- Competition participant waiver
- Media release
- Event ticket terms
- Athlete profile consent language

These drafts are not legal advice and must be reviewed by a qualified attorney
before production use.

Never store completed waivers, identification documents, signatures, private
addresses, or private participant records here.
